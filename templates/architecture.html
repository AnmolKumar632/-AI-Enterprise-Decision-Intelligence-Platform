Platform Architecture

From Data Sources to Model Improvement

A continuous intelligence loop: customer conversations and enterprise data flow through AI models, a decision engine, and scenario simulation — ending in business action that feeds back into model improvement.

Data Sources
Operational systems, customer interactions, sales, finance, and CRM data
AI Base Call Center
Transcribes and analyzes customer voice conversations
Customer / Conversation Intelligence
Sentiment, intent, complaints, churn risk, and agent performance signals
Enterprise Data Layer
Unified, cleaned, versioned business and conversation data
ML / AI Models
AutoML, forecasting, churn, risk, and anomaly models with explainability
Decision Engine
Optimizes across predictions, constraints, and business objectives
Scenario Simulation
Models alternative futures with cost, risk, and impact projections
Recommendation Engine
Actionable, evidence-backed recommendations with confidence scores
Executive Decision Center
KPIs, forecasts, risk, and AI recommendations on one decision surface
Business Action
Executives act — reallocate capacity, change strategy, adjust operations
Outcome / Feedback
Real-world results are captured and compared against predictions
Model Improvement
Continuous learning loop sharpens every model in the pipeline
