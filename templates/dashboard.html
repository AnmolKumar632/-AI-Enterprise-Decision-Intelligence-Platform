Workspace Brief & Guidelines

Welcome to the AI Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform. This workspace acts as a central control room for translating raw operational statistics into strategic intelligence.

Recommended Workflow:
  1. Navigate to Datasets and upload your business spreadsheet (CSV/Excel).
  2. Clean the dataset using the One-click pre-processing engine.
  3. Inspect statistical properties and correlations in the Automated EDA panel.
  4. Train optimized classifiers or regressors in the AutoML Engine.
  5. Run forecasts or risk audits, ask NLP questions, and generate reports.
